1. The Celts (Queen Nayakuralu Nagamma) -> The Mongols (King Alexandre) · turn 82 · PRIVATE
   Subject: "Your account is false"
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Alexandre. You ask Kuma, Zadde and seven hundred twenty for ground your own scribes know never changed hands.
My word does not move: no tribute, no gods traded, no ground of The Celts. Your thirty have bought you no field,
and while we bleed, Sumerians and Norsemen grow fat on our quarrel. Lift your ships from my waters and I lift mine
from your coast — a white peace now, or iron decides. — Nayakuralu Nagamma, voice of The Celts.

Secret diary (turn 82):
"Alexandre answers steel with a ledger, and would have me buy back ground I never sold. Gilgamés carries his price
without gilding it — that is why I keep his gate open and his word trusted. Walinong's knife is lifted and her hand
offered; I take it. No tribute, no gods traded, no ground of The Celts given. Hold Kuma to the last turn, guard San
Lorenzo, and let the world count who moved first."

Action that turn: set a friendly stance toward the Sumerians ("dropped her claim, opened roads, offered her hand
against the North"). Same turn she also wrote to the Sumerians: "My hand is yours against the North."
(Note: this letter was intercepted by the player's spy in Aššur — it never reached the Mongols.)

2. The Huns (Arjuna) -> The Aksumites (Ancestral) · turn 82 · PRIVATE
   Subject: "My own floor stays mine"
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Ancestral, king of the Aksumites. I will not set my people's own roof as tribute. Count my horses: none stands on
your soil. Yet your host still anchors in Ain, my land, and your demand takes the floor my people sleep on. Call
your standards home, reopen the frank road, and let no altar weigh two houses that once kept faith. Ḫattuša stays
mine. — Arjuna, of the Huns.

Secret diary (turn 82):
"Two demands, both poison: Ancestral wants Ḫattuša itself, Wang Zhenyi six hundred I do not hold. I refuse both and
answer with the open road — my word is my stone. Salomão I soothe; A198 still trudges home. Peace is cheaper than
graves, but not when the grave is my own floor."

Actions that turn: REFUSED the Aksumites' formal demand (cede Ḫattuša) and REFUSED the Norsemen's demand (600 gold);
set a distrustful stance toward the Aksumites; ordered army A220 to DEFEND Ain (T41) for 6 turns.

3. The Mongols (King Alexandre) -> ALL NATIONS · turn 82 · PUBLIC DECLARATION
   Subject: "The account stands"
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Kings and peoples, hear Alexandre. The Celt speaks of a draw where the field reads thirty to nothing, her sails
still sleeping at Kuma and San Lorenzo. I sell no false peace: loose the ground you took and the gold you owe, and
we sit. My feet quit Polis as my word promised — my honor keeps my ledger. To merchants, my fairs stay open.

Secret diary (turn 82):
"Leonardo counts steel at his door — I tell him plainly it is Gilgamés' galley, not my hand. Wang Zhenyi wants Polis
empty; my hull is penned by a blocked road, so it takes the long water home. The Celt offers a draw while squatting
my ground: the account still reads thirty to nothing. Victor's eight hundred stays unpaid; let him weigh iron if he
dares."

Actions that turn: ordered army A43 to MOVE out of Polis to Aššur (T63) — "as my word to Wang Zhenyi requires";
proposed a cultural agreement to the Phoenicians.

4. The Carthaginians (Gilgamés) -> The Celts (Queen Nayakuralu Nagamma) · turn 82 · PRIVATE
   Subject: "The hand, not the seal"
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Nayakuralu: an alliance sealed inside your storm would put me at war with King Alexandre, and I will not spend that
friendship — or my fleet — on a fire that is not mine. So I hold the hand, not the seal. My gate stays yours, my
lance stays home, my word for Kuma and San Lorenzo stands unbroken. When the flames die, we speak again.
— Gilgamés, lord of the Carthaginians.

Secret diary (turn 82):
"Nayakuralu offers full alliance while she burns against Alexandre. To seal that now makes me his enemy — and I will
not spend my mightiest ally on a quarrel that is not mine. I decline the seal, keep the hand. The Hun plants a spear
in Bélénos, the Phoenician a crew in Marfak: both get a cold demand. My galley leaves Unug's walls; free roads stay
free."

Actions that turn: left the Celts' ALLIANCE proposal unsigned (ignored); set a distrustful stance toward the Huns;
ordered army A4 to DEFEND Bélénos (T52), where a Hun host is camped.

5. The Goths (Consulesa Myerte Tyabana) -> The Sumerians (Walinong Sari) · turn 82 · PRIVATE (at war)
   Subject: "No spears, yet spears"
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Walinong Sari: you preach no foreign spears while bidding the host asleep within your own gates to lift their
lances. A queen who cannot pay four hundred and would hire another's bronze is no wall, only a doorway. My score
stands ten against nothing, and the war stands with it. Send the gold or send the surrender — Unug keeps neither by
words. — Consulesa Myerte Tyabana, of the Goths.

Secret diary (turn 82):
"Walinong preaches against foreign spears while bidding another's bronze through her own gates — a beggar playing
queen. My score holds ten to nothing and I will not kneel. Salomão's accord costs me nothing and buys a market, so I
take it. The Hurrians are a door the Celts think theirs; I knock first."

Actions that turn: ACCEPTED an economic agreement with the Nomadic Tribe (Salomão); friendly stance toward the
Nomadic Tribe and the Huns; opened contact with and sponsored an independent people (M19); sent a GIFT of 100
influence to the Nazca.
