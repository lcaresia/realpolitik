Living Nations

What they wrote you. What they wrote themselves.

Every turn, each nation reads a dossier with only what it knows, so fog of war holds. It writes a secret diary, keeps memory and feelings (affection, trust, fear, anger), and writes letters in the voice of its culture. It bluffs, stalls, feigns misunderstanding, betrays, and collects on old promises. Nothing is forced, and ignoring a rival has consequences. Letters cite only resources, cities, leaders and events that exist in your game.

Espionage

Some letters never arrive.

Your spies intercept private letters. An intercepted letter is never delivered. AI nations spy on each other too. In turn 74 of a real game, the Celts wrote to the Sumerians about the North, and the Norse leader’s own spy read it.

The Letters tab lives inside the game's own Intelligence screen. Each spy reads at most one letter per turn.

Staged capture. For this screenshot the spy was created and the interception forced with a developer command. The mechanic is real and happens on its own in play.

Your council

Twelve chairs. Six speak each turn.

Eleven ministries plus the Hand of the Throne. You answer up to eight times a turn, they react, their regard for you moves, and you can dismiss any of them. Rival nations keep councils of twelve too.

Real screenshot, turn 82. The meeting cost US$ 0.0020 in AI fees.
Money

Every empire prints its own coin. Every point of interest costs you.

A Central Bank with four tabs: Exchange, Policy, Cycle, Your currency. Set the rate by hand, or let the Taylor rule do it. Production feeds inflation, inflation feeds interest, interest slows production, so nobody grows forever. Exchange rates follow economic strength and inflation, every transfer between empires is converted, and the effects show up in the game's native bonus and penalty tooltips.

Trade blockades

Name a toll. Watch the caravans choose.

Each trading post can be Free, Toll or Blocked, per foreign empire. Routes weigh paying against detouring, and the preview tells you how many do which. Blocking files a real diplomatic grievance with the victim. The AI taxes and blocks you back.
