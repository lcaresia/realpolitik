REALPOLITIK: LIVING NATIONS FOR HUMANKIND
=========================================

REQUIREMENTS
- HUMANKIND on Windows (Steam or Epic), single-player.
- Internet only to activate the license and for Realpolitik's AI.

INSTALL
- Setup: run Realpolitik_Setup.exe. It finds the game and installs BepInEx if needed.
  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
- Manual (zip): close the game, extract the zip INTO the game folder (the one with
  Humankind.exe) and accept merging the BepInEx folder.

ACTIVATE
1. Open the game -> main menu -> Realpolitik -> License.
2. Paste your key (RPLN-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX) and click Activate.
   Valid on up to 3 PCs. To move to another PC, use "Release this PC" on the old one.
3. Pick an AI provider on the left (OpenRouter is the easiest: one login, no key).
Without a license everything else works (currency, Central Bank, tolls, 16 empires)
and nations use the game's native AI.

UPDATE
Install the new version over the old one. Settings, keys and saves are kept.

UNINSTALL
Windows Settings -> Apps -> "Realpolitik: Living Nations for HUMANKIND".
The uninstaller asks whether to keep your settings and keys. Saves are never deleted.

STEAM DECK / LINUX
Use the zip. In Steam: HUMANKIND -> Properties -> Launch options:
  WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%

PROBLEMS
- The mod doesn't load: look for "CurrencyMod" and "Núcleo carregado" in BepInEx\LogOutput.log.
- Your antivirus removed winhttp.dll: restore it (it is the BepInEx loader) and add an exception.
- Saves with more than 10 empires only open with the mod installed.
- Lost your key: realpolitik-living-nations.pages.dev/download.html (purchase e-mail +
  Stripe receipt number).
