Free and open source
Realpolitik: Living Nations is free and open source, released under the MIT License (full text below). You may use, copy, modify and share it. Source code and releases: github.com/lcaresia/realpolitik

Unofficial mod
This is an unofficial fan-made mod for HUMANKIND. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amplitude Studios or SEGA. You need your own copy of HUMANKIND. Single-player only. Use it at your own risk and keep backups of your saves.

AI providers
AI Diplomacy uses an AI provider you choose and set up with your own account, sign-in or API key (or a local model on your PC). These are third-party services: their terms and prices apply, and any usage fees are between you and that provider. A spending cap per game is built in.

Your data
Only each nation's dossier (the game state it knows: map, relations, letters and memory) is sent, and only to the AI provider in use. Nothing else leaves your PC, and the mod has no telemetry. Keys and sign-ins are stored encrypted on this PC with Windows DPAPI, in BepInEx\config\credenciais.

Open-source components
The mod ships with open-source components: BepInEx, HarmonyX, MonoMod and Mono.Cecil (MIT) and UnityDoorstop (LGPL-2.1). Their licenses, in the "licencas" folder, apply to those components.
