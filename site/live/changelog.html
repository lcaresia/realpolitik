Changelog

1.6.0 — October 28, 2026

  • Multiplayer (early): the economy and trade now work in online games. Currency, inflation, interest, exchange rates, the Central Bank, trade blockades, tolls and trade hubs run the same way on every player's computer.
  • Every player needs the same Realpolitik version. The host sends its economy state and settings when the game starts or loads; if a player does not have the mod or has another version, the mod stays off for everyone and shows a notice.
  • Your interest, currency name and toll choices reach all players as game orders, so everyone sees the same thing.
  • Report a problem (F4) now also works in multiplayer.
  • Still single-player only: AI nations, letters, Congress, council, blocs and alliances.
  • Single-player: trade hub memory (supply crises, epidemics) is now kept in the save, so a reloaded game continues exactly the same way.

1.5.5 — October 20, 2026

  • Council: the Foreign Affairs minister now pays attention to independent peoples, almost like nations but smaller and much less important. When no nation needs attention, they bring up a vassal or annexation within reach, a rival taking over a people, a cheap treaty you can sign or a people you already sponsor, naming them. AI nations' ministers follow the same rules.

1.5.4 — October 20, 2026

  • Diplomacy Trade tab: Your Trading Posts no longer disappears when you hold many resources and the other empire sells nothing; the tab now grows to fit it.
  • Council: appointing a minister checks the influence you have right now, not the amount from the start of the turn.
  • Council: ministers know what you already did this turn before summoning them (laws chosen, deals proposed, diplomatic actions) and no longer advise it again.
  • AI nations count the messenger delay: they judge your reaction to a letter only from the turn after it reaches you, instead of reading your silence as defiance.
  • Mail: letters that arrived on earlier turns are no longer marked New once you have read them.
  • Foreign policy panel: doctrine names (Opportunist, Imperialist...) are now translated into every language.

1.5.3 — October 18, 2026

  • Council ministers now speak as people, not neutral experts: their post, traits and ambition shape what they argue for, and every minister who speaks takes a side instead of handing the choice back to you.
  • The Central Bank warning badge goes away once you open the Central Bank, and only comes back if the warning changes.
  • Council: hiring or firing a minister no longer shows up as the title of the Summon card; the result now appears briefly in the Ministers header.
  • World Congress motion panel: the president tag no longer runs out of the panel.
  • Diplomacy Trade tab: Your Trading Posts no longer overlaps Your Resources when you hold many resources.

1.5.2 — October 15, 2026

  • Council candidates no longer wait forever: every ~10 turns (scaled by game speed) one of a vacant post's three candidates gets tired of waiting and someone new takes the place, one at a time. The meeting notes it and the vacant post card shows who left and who joined.

1.5.1 — October 14, 2026

  • Keyboard shortcuts for every bottom-bar button: F2 Mail, F3 Alliances, F4 Report a problem, F6 Realpolitik screen, F7 Council, F8 Central Bank. Each tooltip shows its key.
  • The Alliances button always shows, and Report a problem opens like a system screen and returns you to the map.
  • The Council now sees your real treasury mid-turn; the money tooltip shows inflation, interest and interest income on separate lines.
  • AI nations know a refused demand cannot be paid until they withdraw it or make a new one.
  • Names instead of internal codes, proper plurals, neutral wording, clearer buttons and second-click confirmations for destructive actions.
  • Bank currency ranking always shows your position; Resources chart scale fixed; "Create another group" always available in the Mail.
  • Updating: run the new Setup over the old one, no need to uninstall first.

1.5.0 — October 14, 2026

  • Report a problem: a permanent button on the bottom bar sends all the game and mod logs (including the previous session, to catch freezes) and gives you a code to share on Discord or GitHub. Keys, e-mails and your Windows user name are removed first.
  • Council in the Neolithic: the bottom bar now shows from turn 1, and F7 opens or closes the Council at any time.
  • Council meeting interval can be changed on the Council screen (automatic, 3 to 20 turns, or only by audience); automatic follows the game speed.
  • The Council's red "!" only lights up for demands that still wait for you, and the Council screen shows why it called you.
  • When you write about a matter twice, AI nations can mark their older letter as "Already settled" instead of waiting for a second reply.
  • Fixed the Relations History popup hidden behind the Economy panel and minister portraits showing in previous meetings.

1.4.0 — October 13, 2026

  • Nations keep one fixed, realistic country name for the whole game ("Goths of Valdoria"), in the mod screens and in the game's own screens and map.
  • Foreign-policy doctrines (13, from international-relations research) and ambitions give each AI nation a goal; each nation records how it reads the others, shown in its diplomacy profile.
  • Deals ledger: AI nations record and close the bargains they strike by letter; you can record one too with the new "Deal" letter type. Requests waiting for an answer are put to the AI as trades.
  • Blocs: free-text motions (3 per member, 2-turn vote, 10-turn resolutions), the president only breaks ties, expulsion with 2/3, and a book of accusations. The mod never judges or enforces anything.
  • World Congress: free-text motions for every nation, with the same rules; passed resolutions are only recorded.
  • Trade network: hubs and grand routes with their effects, cargo of faith and culture, dependency map in the AI dossier, shorter labels and a legend on the route map.
  • AI nations choose their civics and know their religion and laws; council follows the leader's orders and shows the newest exchange on top.
  • Mail: colored relation dot before every name, group name on group messages, replies always private, voted options marked.
  • Up to 5 AI letters per turn; AI nations recover on their own when the Codex login check fails for a moment.
  • Fixed a one-second freeze every two seconds in the trade view, an invisible-interface bug and a crash on hot reload; ESC safety net.

1.3.1 — October 11, 2026

  • AI nations no longer drop to a broken fallback provider when the Codex login check is slow; a rejected key sits out for an hour.
  • Prompt-injection guard: letter text is fenced, and letters that talk to the AI instead of a leader are flagged.
  • Money tooltip: negatives in red, balance interest, tolls received and paid.
  • Trade view: relation, exchange rate and trade balance under each empire; resources chart shows money per turn.
  • Leader names next to the people across the mod screens; civics emblems name the empire; bloc president term shown.
  • The Merchant era star now measures your treasury.
  • Council candidates each get their own portrait and show their starting numbers and tendencies.

1.3.0 — October 11, 2026

  • Blocs and a new Alliances screen: found a bloc, elect a president, vote on common tariffs, members and the Congress vote; no tolls between members and mutual defence.
  • Group chats in the Diplomatic Mail, for you and the AI nations.
  • Councils start with only the Hand: appoint ministers from 3 candidates with painted portraits, full traits and starting numbers; veterans earn bonuses in their area.
  • Economy: AI nations set cash floors so they stop buying into debt, idle money earns interest, and 10% of route upkeep goes to the territories crossed.
  • AI nations answer letters again: reminders of unanswered letters, and your group messages ask for an answer.
  • Fixes: hidden buttons in the Mail and the Central Bank (automatic/manual interest).

1.2.0 — October 8, 2026

  • Realpolitik is now free and open source (MIT).
  • License keys removed; AI Diplomacy works for everyone.
  • Source and builds on GitHub.

1.0.1 — October 7, 2026

  • The in-game menu button and settings screen now carry the mod's name, Realpolitik, instead of “AI Diplomacy” (all 5 languages).

1.0.0 — October 6, 2026

  • Living Nations: AI empires that read a fog-of-war dossier, keep a secret diary, remember, and write letters.
  • 32 kinds of real in-game actions, including army orders and World Congress votes (Together We Rule DLC).
  • Diplomatic Mail, a Letters tab in diplomacy, and spies that intercept private letters.
  • Your council of twelve; AI nations keep councils too.
  • Own currency per empire, a Central Bank and the production → inflation → interest cycle.
  • Trade posts: Free, Toll or Blocked, per foreign empire.
  • Up to 16 empires with the optional MoreEmpires plugin.
  • 5 languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and German.
