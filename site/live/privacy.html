Privacy

Last updated October 8, 2026

In the game

When AI Diplomacy is on, each computer nation's dossier is sent to the AI provider you choose: what that nation sees on the map, its relations, letters and memory. Nothing personal is sent — not your name, not your Steam account.

Your API keys and sign-ins are stored encrypted on your own PC. They are never sent to us. Each provider processes requests under its own privacy policy.

On this website

This site sets no cookies. We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits: it records anonymous page views, the referring site, browser and device type and approximate country, plus clicks on the main buttons. It uses no cookies and builds no personal profiles.

Downloads

The mod is free and has no accounts, purchases or license keys, so we collect no customer data. Downloads are served by GitHub under its own privacy policy. If you choose a local model, AI Diplomacy runs on your own PC and the dossiers never leave it.
