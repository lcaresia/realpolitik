Privacy

Draft · last updated October 2026

In the game

When AI Diplomacy is on, each computer nation's dossier is sent to the AI provider you choose: what that nation sees on the map, its relations, letters and memory. Nothing personal is sent — not your name, not your Steam account.

Your API keys and sign-ins are stored encrypted on your own PC. They are never sent to us. Each provider processes requests under its own privacy policy.

On this website

This site sets no cookies. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or personal profiles.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.

To deliver your license and downloads we keep: your email, the Stripe order and payment IDs, the order status (active, refunded or disputed), and for each activated PC a random ID, a short machine nickname (not your name) and the dates it was activated and last checked. The license key itself is stored only in protected form. We use this data only to deliver the product, prevent abuse and answer support requests.
